# Five-minute demo

1. Open the showcase and introduce **Diatonic OS by Chromatix Studios**, built by **Drew Fortini, Founder**.
2. Show the four shared stages: observe, plan, coordinate, verify.
3. Explain that the infrastructure cards are synthetic. Run the built-in sample and show the content digest and verification result.
4. Choose a small file. Repeat the workflow and download the JSON receipt. Explain that two reads were checked and no production file was moved or uploaded.
5. Choose a video or audio file. Show the local preview and browser-reported duration. State clearly that transcription, generation, and production media processing are separate integrations.
6. Open the SDK and RMM repositories and their CI runs. Explain how the same foundation is intended to coordinate native services without replacing their authority.
7. Finish with what is verified, what failed during development, and what remains pending.

A successful demo produces a receipt with `scope: local-browser-demo`, `verified: true`, zero provider calls, and zero production actions. Content hashes are evidence of identity, not of semantic correctness or legal ownership.
